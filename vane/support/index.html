2. 怎么恢复购买？家人能一起用吗？

  • 恢复购买：「设置 › Vane Pro」里点「恢复购买」。前提是 App Store 登录的是当初购买的那个 Apple 账户。重装或换一台 iPhone / iPad 之后都这样找回。
  • 家人共享：Vane Pro 支持家人共享。你在 Apple 的「家人共享」里开启了购买项目共享，家庭成员用自己的设备打开相风即可，必要时同样点一次「恢复购买」。
  • 显示「等待批准」：这次购买需要家长批准（Apple 的「购买前询问」），批准后会自动解锁。
  • 退款由 Apple 按其规则处理，入口在 reportaproblem.apple.com。

3. 为什么发到某个平台之后不动了？

各个平台对实况的态度不一样：有的原样播放，有的只收视频，有的会把实况压成一张静态图。相风的做法是先问你要发到哪，再按那里的规则做——那里不播实况，就换成它认的格式（视频、GIF 或安卓动态照片），并在保存时说明为什么。

  • 先看平台规则页（「设置 › 平台规则」）。每条规则都附来源与核对日期；平台改了规则、我还没来得及更新的话，欢迎来信告诉我。
  • 再用体检。把那张不动的实况交给体检，它会告诉你问题出在哪；用 Vane Pro 的一键修复可以另存一份修好的，原来那张不动。
  • 确认保存时选对了目的地。同一段视频，给「照片」做的和给某个平台做的，可能不是同一种文件。
  • 有些平台发布时有自己的开关或压缩设置，也会影响最终效果；这部分在对方 App 里，相风管不到。

4. 安卓朋友看到的是一张静态图

实况是 Apple 的格式，安卓手机大多不认。两个办法：

  • 做成安卓动态照片（Vane Pro）：目的地选「安卓（动态照片）」。它是一张内嵌视频的 JPEG，要按文件发——比如隔空投送、「文件」，或在 Telegram 里以文件发送；当作普通图片发，多半会被压成静态图。
  • 直接发 WhatsApp 或 Telegram：目的地选对应的 App，相风会按它的规则做成对方能播的格式。

5. 做好的实况存到哪了？

  • 保存后，成品在系统「照片」App 里，和你自己拍的照片放在一起。
  • 默认会带上源视频的拍摄时间（见第 9 条），所以它会排在拍摄那一天，而不是今天——在「最近项目」里往前翻，或按日期找。
  • 给某个平台做的成品也可能是视频或 GIF，而不是实况，在「照片」里对应的类型下能找到。

6. 为什么需要相册权限？

相风只和相册打交道，并且只申请「仅添加照片」这一项权限，从不申请完整相册访问：

  • 挑视频不需要权限：用的是系统照片选择器，只有你选中的那几项会交给相风。
  • 保存只要「仅添加照片」：只能往相册里写，不能读。
  • 在原图上编辑也不需要权限：它走的是照片编辑扩展，由系统「照片」App 把那一张交给相风、保存也由「照片」处理，修改随时可以在「照片」里还原。App 里的图库页同样是系统选择器。

相风从不申请完整相册访问，也不会自行读取你的相册。所有处理都在设备上完成，不上传——详见隐私政策第 4 条。

7. 设成锁屏壁纸之后不动

锁屏壁纸动不动，是 iOS 按一套未公开的条件决定的，普通实况设成壁纸经常不动。

  • 「设置 › 实验室」里有锁屏版本，会尝试做一份更容易在锁屏上动起来的版本。
  • 它是实验功能，默认关闭，不承诺结果：同一份文件在不同设备、不同系统版本上可能表现不同。
  • 实验室里的其他功能（补帧、超分等）也是同样的性质。试了之后效果如何，欢迎来信告诉我。

8. 能不能不从 App 里开始做？

  • 分享扩展：在「照片」或别的 App 里选中视频，点分享，选相风。
  • 照片编辑扩展：在「照片」里编辑一张实况时，从扩展里选相风。
  • 快捷指令：「做成实况」按目的地的默认规则把一段视频做好，存进「照片」，可以放进你自己的自动化里。

这几条路同样只在本机处理。

9. 做出来的实况带着拍摄地点吗？

默认带。相风会把源视频的拍摄时间、地点与机身参数写到新的实况上，让它排在拍摄那天、显示在拍摄地点。源视频里本来没有的信息，不会补也不会猜。

不想带拍摄时间与地点，在设置里关掉「保留原拍摄时间与地点」再保存。这些信息只写进你自己相册里的文件，不会发给我们；但它们跟着文件走，发出去之前请留意。

10. 作品存在哪？怎么彻底清空？

  • 作品（草稿）以「配方 + 原片副本」的形式存在相风自己的沙盒里，方便以后接着改。在作品列表里删除，副本会一起删掉，「照片」里的不动。
  • 临时文件：相风每次启动时会清理残留的部分。
  • 全部作品与设置：删除相风即可。
  • 已经存进「照片」的成品由「照片」App 管理，删除相风不会带走它们，请在「照片」里删除。
  • 购买记录属于你的 Apple 账户，由 Apple 管理。

我们不持有你的任何数据，所以没有需要向我们申请删除的东西。详见隐私政策。

2. Restoring purchases and Family Sharing

  • Restore: go to Settings › Vane Pro and tap Restore Purchase, signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought it with. This is also how you get it back after reinstalling or on a new iPhone or iPad.
  • Family Sharing: Vane Pro supports Family Sharing. If purchase sharing is on in your Apple Family Sharing settings, family members just open Vane on their own devices — tapping Restore Purchase once if needed.
  • "Waiting for approval" means the purchase needs a parent's approval (Apple's Ask to Buy); it unlocks automatically once approved.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under their rules at reportaproblem.apple.com.

3. Why doesn't it move after I post it?

Platforms treat Live Photos differently: some play them as they are, some accept only video, and some flatten them into a still. Vane asks where you're posting first and follows that platform's rules. Where Live Photos don't play, it makes the format the platform does accept — a video, a GIF, or an Android Motion Photo — and tells you why when you save.

  • Start with the platform rules page (Settings › Platform rules). Every rule lists its source and the date it was verified. If a platform has changed and I haven't caught up yet, please let me know.
  • Then use Check. Give it the Live Photo that won't move and it will tell you where the problem is. With Vane Pro, one-tap repair saves a fixed copy and leaves the original alone.
  • Make sure you picked the right destination when saving. The same video made for Photos and made for a given platform may not be the same kind of file.
  • Some platforms have their own upload switches or compression settings that affect the result; those live in the other app, outside Vane's reach.

4. My Android friends see a still photo

Live Photo is an Apple format, and most Android phones don't recognise it. Two ways around it:

  • Make an Android Motion Photo (Vane Pro): choose "Android (Motion Photo)" as the destination. It's a JPEG with the video inside, and it must be sent as a file — for example with AirDrop, Files, or as a file in Telegram. Sent as an ordinary photo, it will usually be flattened to a still.
  • Post straight to WhatsApp or Telegram: choose that app as the destination, and Vane makes a format it can play.

5. Where did my Live Photo go?

  • Once saved, results are in the Photos app, alongside the photos you took yourself.
  • By default they carry the source video's capture date (see question 9), so they sort to the day it was filmed, not today — scroll back in Recents or look by date.
  • A result made for a particular platform may be a video or a GIF rather than a Live Photo; look under the matching media type in Photos.

6. Why does it need photo access?

Vane only works with your photo library, and requests only "Add Photos Only" — never full library access:

  • Picking videos needs no permission: it uses the system photo picker, so only the items you select are handed to Vane.
  • Saving needs only "Add Photos Only": it can write to your library but cannot read it.
  • Editing an original needs no permission either: it goes through the Photos editing extension, where the Photos app hands the item to Vane and saves the result itself; edits can be reverted in Photos at any time. The library view inside the app is the system picker too.

Vane never requests full library access and never reads your library on its own. All processing happens on the device and nothing is uploaded — see section 4 of the Privacy Policy.

7. It doesn't move as a Lock Screen wallpaper

Whether a Lock Screen wallpaper moves is decided by iOS using undocumented checks, and ordinary Live Photos often stay still there.

  • Settings › Experiments has a Lock Screen version that tries to make a copy more likely to move on the Lock Screen.
  • It is experimental, off by default, and comes with no promises: the same file may behave differently on different devices and iOS versions.
  • The other experiments (frame interpolation, upscaling, and so on) are in the same spirit. If you try them, I'd like to hear how it went.

8. Can I start without opening the app?

  • Share extension: select a video in Photos or another app, tap Share, and choose Vane.
  • Photos editing extension: while editing a Live Photo in Photos, pick Vane from the extensions.
  • Shortcuts: "Make Live Photo" turns a video into a Live Photo using a destination's default rules and saves it to Photos — ready to drop into your own automations.

All of these process everything on the device too.

9. Do my Live Photos carry the location?

By default, yes. Vane writes the source video's capture date, place, and camera details into the new Live Photo, so it sorts to the day it was filmed and shows where it was taken. Information the source video doesn't have is never filled in or guessed.

To leave out the capture date and place, turn off "Keep original date & place" in Settings before saving. These details are written only into files in your own library and never sent to us — but they travel with the file, so keep that in mind before posting.

10. Where are projects stored? How do I erase everything?

  • Projects (drafts) are kept in Vane's own sandbox as a recipe plus a copy of the original, so you can keep editing later. Deleting a project in the project list deletes its copy too; nothing in Photos is touched.
  • Temporary files: Vane cleans up any leftovers each time it launches.
  • All projects and settings: delete the app.
  • Results already saved to Photos are managed by the Photos app and aren't removed when you delete Vane — delete them in Photos.
  • Purchase records belong to your Apple Account and are managed by Apple.

We hold none of your data, so there is nothing to ask us to delete. See the Privacy Policy.
