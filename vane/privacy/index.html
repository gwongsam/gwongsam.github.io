一句话摘要

  • 我们没有服务器。你的视频、实况与作品不会被上传，也没有任何地方可以上传到我们这里。所有处理都在你的设备上完成。
  • 没有账号、没有广告、没有统计、没有追踪、没有任何第三方 SDK。本应用只用 Apple 提供的系统框架，自身不发起任何网络请求。

1. 谁在提供这款应用

相风（英文名 Vane，下称「本应用」）由个人开发者 gwongsam（下称「我们」）开发与发行，运行于 iPhone 与 iPad。它用来把视频做成实况（Live Photo）、重新编辑已有的实况，并按你要发往的平台把成品做成那里能播放的格式。

本政策说明本应用如何处理你的信息。贯穿全文的前提是：我们没有后端服务器，你的视频、实况与作品不会到达我们手中。下文描述的，实际是数据如何在你自己的设备上流动。

2. 应用处理哪些数据

以下数据在你使用过程中产生或被读取，全部保留在你的设备上：

类别具体内容
你选中的视频与实况为了预览、编辑、体检和导出而读取的画面、声音与元数据——包括源视频自带的拍摄时间、地点与机身参数（如果有）
配乐你为作品挑的音频，来自「文件」里的音乐或另一段视频的声音
作品（草稿）你的编辑配方（选段、封面、裁切、调色、相框、文字、速度、声音等参数），以及一份原片副本，以便以后接着改
端侧分析结果封面推荐、显著性裁切等在设备上算出来的结果，只用于给你建议，不离开设备
设置与偏好「常发的地方」、「保留原拍摄时间与地点」开关、实验室开关等界面偏好
购买状态是否已购买 Vane Pro——由 Apple 的 StoreKit 在本机告诉本应用（见第 7 条）

本应用不索取、不收集：姓名、身份证件号码、手机号码、电子邮件、支付信息、通讯录、日历、位置（除源视频里本来就有的地点信息）、广告标识符、设备指纹。本应用不请求相机、麦克风或定位权限。本应用没有账号体系，无需注册或登录。

3. 数据存放在哪里

全部在你自己的设备上，分三处：

  • 本应用的沙盒容器。作品以「配方 + 原片副本」的形式存在这里。处理过程中的临时文件，本应用会在每次启动时清理掉残留的部分。
  • 本机偏好设置。设置与偏好存在系统的 UserDefaults 里，只在这台设备上。
  • 系统相册。你保存的实况、视频与 GIF 会写入系统「照片」App。这些文件此后由「照片」App 管理，受你的 iCloud 照片设置影响。

本应用自身不做任何云同步，也不向任何服务器写入数据。存进系统相册的文件是否同步到 iCloud，由你在系统设置里的「iCloud 照片」决定，与本应用无关。

分享扩展与照片编辑扩展（从别的 App 或「照片」里直接调用本应用）以及快捷指令「做成实况」，同样只在本机处理，结果同样存进你的相册。

4. 相册权限各用来做什么

本应用只和相册打交道，并且只申请「仅添加照片」这一项权限，从不申请完整相册访问：

情形需要的权限说明
挑选要做的视频或实况不需要使用系统照片选择器：只有你选中的那几项会交给本应用，其余照片它看不到
保存成品仅添加照片只能往相册里写，不能读你的相册
在原图上编辑（可还原）不需要通过照片编辑扩展完成：由系统「照片」App 把你正在编辑的那一张交给本应用，保存也由「照片」App 处理；修改可以随时在「照片」里还原

本应用内的图库页同样是嵌入式的系统照片选择器，不需要权限。本应用从不申请完整相册访问，也不会自行读取你的相册。「仅添加照片」可以在「设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 照片」里随时更改；关掉之后只是存不进相册，其余照常。

5. 联网：只经由 Apple 的系统服务

本应用不含任何自定义的网络代码，也没有我们的服务器。你的视频、实况与作品不会被本应用发送到任何地方。只有以下情形会产生网络流量，都由 Apple 的系统框架发起：

  • 购买。App 内购买、价格显示与交易记录由 Apple 的 StoreKit 与 App Store 完成（见第 7 条）。
  • 超分模型（仅当你主动下载）。如果你在「设置 › 实验室」里点下载超分模型，系统会从 Apple 下载这个模型文件。这一步只是下载，不上传你的任何内容；不点就不会发生。

平台规则页里的来源链接由系统浏览器打开，不经过本应用。除此之外，本应用不上传内容、不检查更新、不回传日志，也不接入任何广告、统计或归因网络。

发到别的平台这一步由你完成。本应用只把成品存进你的相册，或经系统分享面板交给你选的 App；之后在 WhatsApp、微信、Instagram 等 App 里发布时，适用的是那个平台自己的隐私政策。

6. 做出来的文件里带着什么

  • 默认情况下，本应用会把源视频的拍摄时间、地点与机身参数写到新做的实况上，让它在相册里排在拍摄当天、显示在拍摄地点。
  • 不想带拍摄时间与地点，在设置里关掉「保留原拍摄时间与地点」即可。
  • 源视频里本来没有的信息，本应用不会补、也不会猜。
  • 这些信息只写进你自己相册里的文件，不会发给我们。

这些信息跟着文件一起走。把成品发给别人或上传到网上之前，如果不想带出拍摄地点，请先关掉「保留原拍摄时间与地点」再保存。发布后各平台是否保留这些信息，由平台决定。

10. 数据保留与删除

我们不持有你的数据，因此不存在由我们进行的「保留期」。数据的存续完全由你控制：

  • 作品（草稿）：在本应用里删除即可；删除本应用时，全部作品连同其中的原片副本一并删除。
  • 临时文件：本应用每次启动时自动清理残留的部分。
  • 设置与偏好：删除本应用即清除。
  • 存进系统相册的文件：由「照片」App 管理，删除本应用不会带走它们，请在「照片」里删除；在原图上做的编辑可以在「照片」里还原。
  • 购买记录：属于你的 Apple 账户，由 Apple 管理。

你无需联系我们即可完成上述全部操作，我们也没有任何副本可供删除。

  • We have no servers. Your videos, Live Photos, and projects are never uploaded — there is nowhere to upload them to. Everything is processed on your device.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs. The app uses only Apple's system frameworks and makes no network requests of its own.
  • The only permission requested is "Add Photos Only." Picking videos uses the system photo picker and needs no permission; saving results needs only "Add Photos Only." The app never requests full library access and cannot see anything in your library you haven't selected.
  • Original details stay in your own library. By default the source video's capture date, place, and camera details are written into the new Live Photo. The capture date and place can be turned off in Settings, and none of it is ever sent to us.

1. Who provides this app

Vane (相风 in Chinese, "the app") is developed and published by gwongsam ("we"), an individual developer, and runs on iPhone and iPad. It turns videos into Live Photos, re-edits existing Live Photos, and makes the result in a format that plays on the platform you're posting to.

This policy explains how the app handles your information. The premise running through all of it: we operate no backend servers, so your videos, Live Photos, and projects never reach us. What follows describes how data moves on your own device.

2. What data the app handles

The following is created or read while you use the app. All of it stays on your device:

CategoryDetails
Videos and Live Photos you selectPicture, sound, and metadata read in order to preview, edit, check, and export — including the source video's own capture date, place, and camera details, if it has them
MusicAudio you choose for a project, from music in Files or the sound of another video
Projects (drafts)Your edit recipe (trim, cover, crop, color, frame, text, speed, sound, and so on) plus a copy of the original, so you can keep editing later

The app does not request or collect: names, government ID numbers, phone numbers, email addresses, payment details, contacts, calendars, location (beyond place information already inside your source videos), advertising identifiers, or device fingerprints. It does not ask for camera, microphone, or location permission. The app has no account system; there is nothing to register or sign in to.

3. Where the data lives

All on your own device, in three places:

  • The app's sandbox container. Projects are stored here as a recipe plus a copy of the original. Temporary files created during processing are cleaned up each time the app launches if any are left behind.
  • Local preferences. Settings and preferences are kept in the system's UserDefaults, on this device only.
  • The system photo library. Live Photos, videos, and GIFs you save are written to the Photos app. From then on those files are managed by Photos and follow your iCloud Photos settings.

The app itself does no cloud sync and writes to no server. Whether files saved to the system library sync to iCloud is decided by your iCloud Photos setting, independently of this app.

The Share extension and the Photos editing extension (which let you use the app from other apps or from Photos), and the "Make Live Photo" shortcut, likewise process everything on the device and save results to your library.

4. What each photo permission is for

The app only works with your photo library, and requests only "Add Photos Only" — never full library access:

WhenPermission neededNotes
Picking a video or Live Photo to work onNoneUses the system photo picker: only the items you select are handed to the app, and it cannot see the rest of your library
Saving resultsAdd Photos OnlyCan write to your library but cannot read it
Editing the original (revertible)NoneDone through the Photos editing extension: the Photos app hands over the one item you are editing and saves the result itself; edits can be reverted in Photos at any time

The library view inside the app is also the embedded system photo picker and needs no permission. The app never requests full library access and never reads your library on its own. "Add Photos Only" can be changed at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos; without it, results just can't be saved to your library and everything else works as before.

5. Network access: Apple system services only

The app contains no custom networking code and we run no servers. Your videos, Live Photos, and projects are never sent anywhere by the app. Network traffic happens only in these cases, each issued by Apple's system frameworks:

  • Purchases. In-app purchases, price display, and transaction records are handled by Apple's StoreKit and the App Store (see section 7).
  • Upscaling model (only if you download it). If you tap to download the upscaling model under Settings › Experiments, the system downloads that model file from Apple. It is a download only — none of your content is uploaded — and it never happens unless you tap it.

Source links on the platform rules page open in the system browser, not inside the app. Beyond this, the app uploads no content, checks for no updates, sends back no logs, and connects to no advertising, analytics, or attribution network.

Posting to other platforms is something you do. The app only saves results to your library or hands them, through the system share sheet, to the app you choose. When you then post in WhatsApp, WeChat, Instagram, or elsewhere, that platform's own privacy policy applies.

6. What your files carry

  • By default the app writes the source video's capture date, place, and camera details into the new Live Photo, so it sorts to the day it was filmed and shows where it was taken.
  • To leave out the capture date and place, turn off "Keep original date & place" in Settings.
  • The app never fills in or guesses information the source video doesn't have.
  • These details are written only into files in your own library, and are never sent to us.

This information travels with the file. Before sending a result to someone or posting it online, turn off "Keep original date & place" before saving if you'd rather not reveal where it was filmed. Whether a platform keeps these details after you post is up to that platform.

7. Free version and Vane Pro

  • The app is free to download. The free version makes, edits, and saves one at a time, to any destination — no watermark, no ads, no limits.
  • Vane Pro is a one-time purchase (a non-consumable in-app purchase), not a subscription. It never renews and supports Family Sharing. See the app for the price.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store, and we never see your Apple Account, payment method, or billing details.
  • The app reads purchase records locally through Apple's StoreKit, solely to determine whether you own Vane Pro.
  • Purchases belong to your Apple Account: after reinstalling or moving to another device, sign in with the same account and tap Restore Purchase under Settings › Vane Pro.
  • Refunds, parental approval, and similar matters are handled by Apple under their rules.

8. Diagnostics and crash data

The app includes no crash-reporting or performance-analytics SDK and sends back no logs of its own.

If you have enabled "Share with App Developers" under Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple may provide developers with aggregated, non-identifying crash reports and performance metrics. That data is collected and de-identified by Apple, contains none of your videos, Live Photos, or projects, and can be turned off at any time in Settings.

9. Third parties: what we don't do

Stated item by item, to leave no ambiguity:

  • We do not sell your personal information (including "sale" and "sharing" as defined by US state laws);
  • We do not use or disclose your information for targeted advertising, and the app contains no advertising SDK;
  • We integrate no analytics, event-tracking, A/B testing, crash-analytics, attribution, or profiling service;
  • The app contains no third-party SDKs and uses only Apple's system frameworks;
  • We do not use your videos, Live Photos, or projects to train any model, and make no automated decisions;
  • We receive no information from you, so there is no cross-border transfer initiated by us.

The only third party involved is Apple: in-app purchases and transaction records are handled by the App Store, and the upscaling model is downloaded from Apple (section 5). See the Apple Privacy Policy.

10. Retention and deletion

We hold none of your data, so there is no retention period on our side. How long anything lives is entirely up to you:

  • Projects (drafts): delete them in the app. Deleting the app removes every project along with its copy of the original.
  • Temporary files: cleaned up automatically each time the app launches.
  • Settings and preferences: removed when you delete the app.
  • Files saved to the system library: managed by Photos and not removed when you delete the app — delete them in Photos. Edits made to an original can be reverted in Photos.
  • Purchase records: they belong to your Apple Account and are managed by Apple.

You can do all of the above without contacting us, and we have no copy to delete.

13. Security

  • The app runs in the iOS application sandbox and can reach only its own container and the photos you have authorised.
  • The app has no custom networking code, so there is no channel through which your videos, Live Photos, or projects could be sent out by it.
  • We hold none of your data, so there is no "our database was breached" risk to speak of.
  • Live Photos you make carry the original's capture date and place by default (see section 6) — keep that in mind before sharing.

At the same time, please note: the security of the device itself underpins all of this. Set a passcode, enable Face ID or Touch ID, and keep the system updated. No technical measure can guarantee absolute security.
