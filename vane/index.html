它是什么

What it is

目的地优先
Destination first
把一段视频做成实况，或者重新编辑一张已有的实况。动手之前先选要发到哪——长度、画幅、封面都按那里的规则来；那里不播实况，就换成视频、GIF 或安卓动态照片，并把原因写清楚。
Turn a video into a Live Photo, or re-edit one you already have. Choose the destination before anything else — length, framing, and cover follow that platform's rules. Where Live Photos don't play, you get a video, a GIF, or an Android Motion Photo instead, with the reason spelled out.
一次做好，存到好几处
Make once, save for several
选段、封面、裁切、调色、相框、文字、速度、声音与配乐，一个编辑器里做完。一次保存可以同时出给好几个目的地，每一份各按各的规则。
Trim, cover, crop, color, frames, text, speed, sound and music — all in one editor. A single save can produce versions for several destinations at once, each made to its own rules.
体检：为什么不动
Check: why it won't move
一张实况发出去不动，体检会告诉你卡在哪，并能另存一份修好的。平台规则页列出每条规则的来源与核对日期，不凭感觉。
When a Live Photo arrives as a still, Check tells you where it went wrong and can save a repaired copy. The platform rules page lists a source and a verification date for every rule.

「相风」是风向标的古名——汉代的相风铜乌「遇风乃动」；「相」也是照相的相。界面语言：English、简体中文、繁體中文、日本語、한국어、Deutsch、Español、Português (Brasil)。需要 iOS 26 或更高版本。

The Chinese name, 相风, is an ancient word for a weather vane — the bronze crow of the Han dynasty that "moves when the wind comes." 相 is also the first character of "photograph." Available in English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, German, Spanish, and Portuguese (Brazil). Requires iOS 26 or later.

价格与隐私

Pricing and privacy

免费版一张一张地做、改、存到任何目的地，无水印、无广告、不限次数。Vane Pro 是一次性购买，不是订阅，支持家人共享：解锁批量、拼图 / 串联 / 九宫格、全部相框与色调、配乐、安卓动态照片与一键修复（体检本身免费）。价格以 App 内显示为准。没有账号、没有服务器、没有统计、没有广告、没有追踪：所有处理都在你的设备上完成，你的视频与实况不会被上传。

The free version makes, edits, and saves one at a time, to any destination — no watermark, no ads, no limits. Vane Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and supports Family Sharing. It unlocks batch, collage / sequence / 9-grid, every frame and look, music, Android Motion Photo, and one-tap repair (Check itself stays free). See the app for the price. No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads, no tracking: everything is processed on your device, and your videos and Live Photos are never uploaded.
