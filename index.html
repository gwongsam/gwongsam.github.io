它是什么

What it is

真正的复式记账
Real double-entry
不是「收入减支出」，是每笔都有借有贷、恒等式自校验。资产负债表、利润表、现金流量表三张表同源生成，随时对得上。
Not "income minus expenses" — every transaction has both sides, checked against the accounting identity. The balance sheet, income statement, and cash flow statement all derive from the same postings.
账户建模够认真
Accounts modeled properly
信用卡有真实的账单周期与分期，贷款有借据、还款计划与提前还款，先买后付、储值预付、非现金资产都有各自的模型，不是硬塞进「支出」了事。
Credit cards have real billing cycles and installments; loans have drawdowns, repayment schedules, and early payoff. Buy-now-pay-later, prepaid balances, and non-cash assets each get their own model instead of being flattened into "expenses".
数据在你手里
Your data stays yours
没有服务器、没有账号、没有广告、没有分析 SDK。数据默认只在本机，同步走的是你自己 Apple 账户的 iCloud 私有库，随时可导出完整备份。
No servers, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics SDKs. Data lives on your device by default; syncing goes through the iCloud private database of your own Apple Account, and you can export a complete backup at any time.

免费与会员

Free and Premium

核心记账能力免费且不限记录数量。会员解锁跨设备协作、前瞻分析与重度金融工具。已记录的数据永远可读、可导出、可备份——会员到期只关新建入口，不锁数据。

The core bookkeeping is free and unlimited in record count. Premium unlocks cross-device collaboration, forward-looking analysis, and heavier financial instruments. Data you have already recorded stays readable, exportable, and backup-able forever — expiry closes the "create new" entry points, it never locks your data.
