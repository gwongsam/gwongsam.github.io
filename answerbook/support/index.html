1. 怎么把书翻开？

  • 用手指按住书衣，从右往左推开，就像翻一本真的书。
  • 觉得推不顺手，可以在 App 的「设置」里打开「轻点即可翻开」，之后轻点书就能翻开。
  • 问题写不写都可以。不写也行，心里想着那件事就好。
  • 想换一章，翻开之前在章节里选：全书、感情、工作、学业、自己。不选就是全书。

2. 怎么固定时辰，或者关掉天气？

  • 屋里的光默认跟随时辰：晨、昼、暮、夜。想一直停在某个时辰，到「设置」里关掉「跟随时辰」，再选一个你喜欢的。
  • 起风、下雨、雷雨、下雪这些天气，是在本机按日子生成的，不联网，也不代表你所在地的真实天气。不想要的话，在「设置」里关掉「显示天气」。
  • 换书衣只换书衣和纸，光仍然跟着时辰走。

3. 怎么备份？换新手机怎么办？

留下的页和笔记只存在这台 iPhone 上，App 不做云同步。想留一份，或者搬到新手机：

  1. 在旧手机的「设置」里点「导出备份」，用系统的存储面板把备份文件存到「文件」、iCloud 云盘或别的地方；
  2. 在新手机上装好问页，到「设置」里点「导入备份」，选中那份文件。
  • 如果提示「这个备份来自更新的版本」，先把 App 更新到最新版再导入。
  • 导入没成功时，原来的记录不会变。
  • 备份文件里有你留下的页和写下的笔记，请像保管日记一样保管它。

4. 按住话筒没反应

「按住话筒说」只在你按住话筒的时候听，松开字就留下。说的话在本机转成文字，录音不保存、不上传。没反应的话，依次看看：

  • 麦克风权限：到 iOS「设置 › 问页」里打开麦克风。不打开也没关系，键盘上的话筒一样能说。
  • 第一次用：系统需要先准备本机识别，可能要稍等一下；这一步可能需要联网。
  • 语言：本机识别要支持 App 当前的语言；不支持时，可以先用键盘上的话筒。
  • 需要在真实的 iPhone 上使用。

5. 界面语言怎么换？

问页支持 English、简体中文、繁體中文、日本語、한국어，默认跟随系统语言。只想给问页换一种语言，到 iOS 「设置 › 问页 › 语言」里选。

6. 购买、家人共享与退款

  • 问页是付费下载，一次买下就是全部：没有 App 内购买，没有订阅，七套书衣与藏书票都随 App 一起。
  • 重装或换手机时，用同一个 Apple 账户在 App Store 里重新下载即可，不用再付钱。
  • 支持家人共享：在 Apple 的「家人共享」里开启了购买项目共享，家庭成员可以在 App Store 里免费下载。
  • 退款由 Apple 按其规则处理，入口在 reportaproblem.apple.com。

7. 怎么删掉一页，或者全部清空？

  • 删掉一页：打开那一页，点「删除这一页」。
  • 全部清空：「设置」里点「清空所有记录」。会永久删除，无法恢复；想留一份的话，先导出备份。
  • 删除问页：App 里的全部记录和设置会一起删掉。

我们不持有你的任何数据，所以没有需要向我们申请删除的东西。详见隐私政策。

8. 它能告诉我将来会怎样吗？

不能。问页不是占卜或算命工具，也不预测任何事。书里的句子是为这本书写的原创短句。它能做的，是在你想事情的时候陪你停一下，给你一个听听自己答案的机会。

它也不评估情绪，不提供心理、医疗、法律或理财方面的建议。遇到这类事情，请找相应的专业人士。

9. 如果你正处在很难熬的时候

如果问题里提到伤害自己，书不会翻开一页，而是给出一页求助信息：按 iPhone 的地区设置显示当地的心理援助热线；找不到时，会指向 findahelpline.com。

如果此刻就有危险，请立即拨打当地的急救电话，或者去找身边信任的人。你不必一个人扛着。

1. How do I open the book?

  • Put your finger on the cover and push it open from right to left, as you would a real book.
  • If swiping feels awkward, turn on Tap to open in the app's Settings; then a tap on the book opens it.
  • Writing a question is optional. You can simply keep it in mind.
  • To choose a chapter, pick one before opening: All, Love, Work, Study, or Self. If you don't, it's All.

2. Fixing the time of day, or turning off weather

  • By default the light follows the time of day: dawn, day, dusk, night. To keep one time, turn off Follow the time of day in Settings and choose the one you like.
  • Wind, rain, thunderstorms, and snow are made on the device from the date — no connection, and not your local forecast. If you'd rather not have them, turn off Show weather in Settings.
  • Changing the jacket changes only the jacket and paper; the light still follows the time of day.

3. Backups and moving to a new iPhone

Kept pages and notes live only on this iPhone, and the app does no cloud sync. To keep a copy or move to a new phone:

  1. On the old iPhone, tap Export backup in Settings and use the system save sheet to put the backup file in Files, iCloud Drive, or anywhere you like;
  2. On the new iPhone, install Ask a Page, tap Import backup in Settings, and choose that file.
  • If you're told the backup is from a newer version, update the app first, then import.
  • If an import doesn't go through, your existing pages are left unchanged.
  • The backup file contains your kept pages and the notes you wrote — look after it the way you would a diary.

4. The mic button doesn't respond

Hold to speak listens only while you hold the mic; let go and the words stay. Speech is turned into text on the device, and the audio is never saved or uploaded. If nothing happens, check these in turn:

  • Microphone access: turn on the microphone in iOS Settings › Ask a Page. It's fine without it — the mic on your keyboard works too.
  • First use: the system needs to get on-device recognition ready, which can take a moment and may need a connection.
  • Language: on-device recognition needs to support the app's current language; if it doesn't, use the mic on your keyboard for now.
  • It needs a real iPhone.

5. Changing the language

Ask a Page is available in English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, and Korean, and follows your system language by default. To change it for this app only, go to iOS Settings › Ask a Page › Language.

6. Purchase, Family Sharing, and refunds

  • Ask a Page is a paid download — buy it once and it's all yours: no in-app purchases, no subscription, and all seven jackets and the bookplate are included.
  • After reinstalling or on a new iPhone, download it again from the App Store with the same Apple Account; there's nothing more to pay.
  • Family Sharing is supported: with purchase sharing turned on in Apple's Family Sharing, family members can download it from the App Store at no charge.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under their rules at reportaproblem.apple.com.

7. Deleting a page, or everything

  • One page: open it and tap Delete this page.
  • Everything: tap Erase all pages in Settings. This is permanent and can't be undone; to keep a copy, export a backup first.
  • Deleting the app removes all its pages and settings along with it.

We hold none of your data, so there is nothing to ask us to delete. See the Privacy Policy.

8. Can it tell me what will happen?

No. Ask a Page is not a fortune-telling tool, and it doesn't predict anything. The lines were written for this book. What it offers is a pause while you think something over — a chance to hear your own answer.

It also doesn't assess emotions or give psychological, medical, legal, or financial advice. For those, please turn to a qualified professional.

9. If things are very hard right now

If a question mentions hurting yourself, the book doesn't open a page. Instead it shows a page of help: local helpline numbers based on your iPhone's region, or, where none are listed, a link to findahelpline.com.

If you are in danger right now, call your local emergency number, or reach out to someone you trust. You don't have to carry this alone.
