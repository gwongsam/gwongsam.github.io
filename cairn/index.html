它是什么

What it is

两个方向都算
Counts both ways
倒数报的是还剩多少，进度条像电量条——实心是还没花掉的那部分，从满走到空。纪念日则往上数，100 天、365 天、520 天、1000 天，走过的里程碑一列都留着。
A countdown tells you what's left, on a bar that reads like a battery — the filled part is the time you haven't spent yet. Anniversaries count up instead, and every milestone you pass — 100 days, 365, 1,000 — stays on the list.
一页配得完
Everything on one page
重复规则、多条提醒、每日汇总、农历、进度样式都在同一页。时区锁在事件上——飞一趟美国，家里那件事的数字不会跟着跳。
Repeat rules, multiple reminders, a daily digest, lunar dates, and progress styles all live in one editor. The time zone is pinned to the event, so flying across the world doesn't make the number jump.
数据在你手里
Your data stays yours
没有服务器、没有账号、没有广告、没有分析 SDK。数据存在设备上，同步走的是你自己 Apple 账户的 iCloud 私有库，随时可以导出一份完整的 JSON 备份。
No servers, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics SDKs. Your events live on your device; syncing goes through the iCloud private database of your own Apple Account, and you can export a complete JSON backup at any time.

从已有的日子开始

Start from days you already have

可以从系统日历导入日程、从通讯录导入生日（生日会建成每年重复的事件）。两种导入都只读——Cairn 不会新增、修改或删除你的任何日历事件与联系人，读到的内容也只留在设备上。导入一律是追加而不是替换。

You can import events from your system calendar and birthdays from your contacts (birthdays become yearly repeating events). Both are read-only — Cairn never creates, edits, or deletes a calendar event or a contact, and what it reads stays on your device. Importing always appends rather than replaces.
