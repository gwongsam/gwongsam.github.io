它是什么

What it is

打开就能看
Open and look
常见相机 RAW、HEIC、JPEG、TIFF 与 MOV、MP4 视频。网格、单图、并排对比、全屏查看；RAW 还能把相机的内嵌预览和织光的显影放在一起比。
Common camera RAW formats, HEIC, JPEG, TIFF, plus MOV and MP4 video. Grid, single, side-by-side compare, and full screen — for RAW, put the camera's embedded preview next to Rayloom's own development.
四层调色，原片不动
Four layers, originals untouched
镜前（黑柔、白柔、星光镜、拉丝光……）、光影（曝光与四条曲线）、LUT、调色盘，依次叠加。所有调整存成原片旁边的配方文件，照片本身一个字节不动。
Lens filters (diffusion, star, and streak effects), tone with four curves, LUTs, and a color palette, applied in order. Every adjustment lives in a recipe file next to the photo; the photo itself never changes.
一组素材，一张成片
Many frames, one result
选中一批照片，检查器顶上就是四种玩法：堆栈、延时、景深、导出。哪几张进不了序列、为什么，直接标在网格上，不用跑一半才报错。
Select a batch and the inspector offers four workflows: stack, time-lapse, focus, and export. Frames that can't join the sequence are labelled in the grid with the reason before anything runs.

三种合成

Three ways to combine

玩法做什么
堆栈平均（降噪、抹平水面与人流）、最大值（星轨、光轨、烟花）、星空（按星点旋转对齐，提升暗部信噪比）；素材是 RAW 时可以输出线性 DNG 底片
延时视频普通延时、降噪堆栈、除杂堆栈、星轨视频；1080p 或 4K，30 或 60 fps
景深合成同型 DNG 扫焦序列合成一张前后都清晰的照片，出 HEIF、TIFF、PNG 或 DNG
WorkflowWhat it does
StackAverage (noise reduction, smoothing water and crowds), Maximum (star trails, light trails, fireworks), Star sky (rotational star alignment for cleaner shadows); outputs a linear DNG when the frames are RAW
Time-lapse videoPlain, noise-reduction stack, clean-up stack, or star-trail video; 1080p or 4K at 30 or 60 fps
Focus stackMerges a focus sweep of matching DNG files into one image sharp from front to back, saved as HEIF, TIFF, PNG, or DNG

导出统一走一张面板：HEIF、16 位 TIFF、PNG，位置与命名记得住，⌥⇧⌘E 一键再导，撞名自动加序号。视频同样能套四层滤镜，Apple Log 素材先挂还原表再调色。界面为简体中文，需要 macOS 26 或更高版本。

Every export goes through one panel — HEIF, 16-bit TIFF, or PNG — with locations and naming remembered, ⌥⇧⌘E to repeat, and automatic numbering on name clashes. Video takes the same four layers, with restore LUTs for Apple Log footage. The interface is in Simplified Chinese; requires macOS 26 or later.

价格与隐私

Pricing and privacy

浏览、对比与调色一直免费。导出与合成需要「织光完整版」——一次买断、永久有效、不是订阅；下载后可以先开始 14 天全功能试用。织光不联网：没有账号、没有广告、没有统计，只读你亲自选的文件。

Browsing, comparing, and editing are always free. Exports and combined results need Rayloom Full Version — a one-time purchase, not a subscription — and you can start a 14-day full-featured trial first. Rayloom doesn't go online: no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and it only reads the files you choose.
