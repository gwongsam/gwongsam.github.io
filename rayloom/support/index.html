1. 要花钱吗？免费能做什么？

浏览和调色一直免费，导出需要「织光完整版」。

2. 试用怎么算？重装或换 Mac 之后呢？

  • 试用 14 天，从点下「开始 14 天免费试用」那一刻算起，期间所有功能都能用。不点就不开始计时。
  • 试用是一个价格为 0 的 App 内购买项目，跟着你的 Apple 账户走：每个账户一次，卸载重装、换一台 Mac 都不会重新计时。
  • 到期之后浏览、对比、编辑照常免费，只有导出需要完整版。已经在队列里跑的任务不受影响。
  • 换了 Mac 或重装之后，登录同一个 Apple 账户，在「织光完整版」窗口里点「恢复购买」。提示「这个 Apple 账户没有买过织光完整版」的话，先确认 App Store 登录的是当初购买的那个账户。
  • 显示「等待批准」：这次购买需要家长或管理员批准，批准后会自动解锁。

3. 支持哪些格式？我的 RAW 打不开

类别扩展名
相机 RAWDNG、NEF、CR2、CR3、ARW、RAF、RW2、ORF
图片HEIC、HEIF、JPEG、PNG、TIFF
视频MOV、MP4

RAW 由 macOS 系统解码，织光不自带解码器。所以某台相机的 RAW 能不能显影，取决于你的 macOS 版本是否已经支持这台机型——新机型往往要等系统更新。可以先用「预览」打开同一个文件试试：预览也打不开的，织光同样打不开。

织光只清点文件夹的第一层，不进子文件夹，也跳过隐藏文件。

4. 编辑会改动原片吗？配方存在哪？

不会。镜前、光影、LUT、调色四层的所有调整，都存成原片旁边一个隐藏的配方文件：与原片同名、以点开头、扩展名 .crrecipe（例如 IMG_0001.heic 旁边的 .IMG_0001.crrecipe）。原片一个字节不动。

  • 在访达里按 ⇧⌘. 可以看到这些隐藏文件。把整个文件夹拷到别的 Mac，配方跟着走。
  • 编辑时按「复位」，或把参数都调回初始状态，这个配方文件会被删掉。
  • 只有导出才会生成新文件；只有你在导出面板里明确选择「覆盖原文件」，才会改写原片（见第 11 条）。

5. 用「打开文件」打开的照片，退出后编辑没了

这是有意的。⌘O「打开文件」和从访达双击打开，是「临时打开」：不记路径、不留书签，关掉窗口就不留痕迹。macOS 的沙盒只把这几个文件本身授权给织光，没有授权它们所在的文件夹，所以配方写不进它们旁边，只能留在内存里，退出即清空。

想让编辑保存下来，请用「添加文件夹…」（⇧⌘O）把整个文件夹加成素材来源，或者在退出之前把调好的照片导出。

6. 侧栏里的文件夹变灰了

灰掉并写着「位置暂时不可用」，通常是：外置硬盘没插、网络卷没挂上，或者 iCloud 云盘里的文件夹还没下载到本机。织光不会因此删除这条记录，接回来之后切回织光，它会自动重新检查。

如果文件夹被移动或改了名，请在侧栏右键选「从列表中移除」，再重新添加一次。移除只删除记录，不碰文件夹里的任何文件。

7. 堆栈、延时、景深要什么样的素材？

在网格里选中 2 张以上，右边检查器顶部会出现四种玩法：堆栈、延时、景深、导出。

玩法适合的素材选项
堆栈三脚架上连拍的同一场景，2 到 1500 张，尺寸一致融合方式：平均（降噪、抹平水面与人流）/ 最大值（星轨、光轨、烟花）/ 星空（按星点旋转对齐）；手持或机位有轻微移动时打开「对齐画面」
延时按时间间隔连拍的一组照片，至少能出 2 帧普通延时 / 降噪堆栈 / 除杂堆栈 / 星轨视频；1080p 或 4K；30 或 60 fps。帧按拍摄时间排序
景深只接同型 DNG：同一台相机、同样尺寸与朝向的扫焦序列默认按「近焦 → 远焦」接收；从远往近拍的，打开「这组是从远往近拍的」。顺序跟着网格的排列走

降噪堆栈与除杂堆栈按固定机位融合，移动机位拍的延时会出现重影，这种素材请用「普通延时」。

8. 为什么有几张被排除了？

选好玩法之后，织光会先清点这批素材，进不了序列的会在网格格子上就地标出理由，检查器里也会写「能进这条管线 N / M 张」。常见理由：

  • 视频——合成只接照片；
  • 尺寸不一致——和大多数照片的尺寸不同（比如混进了一张裁过的图）；
  • 与某张是同一张——同名的 RAW 和 JPEG 只保留 RAW；
  • 读不出尺寸 / 不支持的格式——文件损坏，或系统解不了这种 RAW；
  • 单帧超过 1 亿像素也会被拒。

9. 合成出来的成片为什么没有我调的颜色？

堆栈、延时、景深读的是原始素材，不会套用各张照片上的配方——几十张照片各有各的调整，套哪一份都说不通。成片导出之后会出现在侧栏的「成片」里，打开它再进编辑，照常用四层调色。

如果想把一份调色统一烧进一批照片，用「导出」玩法，在导出面板的「配方」里选「统一套一份」，可以挑一份预设或从某张照片拷贝。

10. 导出格式怎么选？DNG 为什么是灰的？

格式什么时候选
HEIF / HEIC直接看、直接分享，体积最小（有损）。批量导出有 8 位与 10 位两档，含 RAW 时默认 10 位
TIFF · 16 位交给别的程序接着修，保留更多层次
PNG需要无损又要通用
DNG · 线性底片只给合成的成片。堆栈要求素材全是同型 RAW（或 RAW 与 TIFF），混进 JPEG、HEIC、PNG 就出不了；景深本来就只接 DNG
  • 单张与批量导出出的是显示域的图，所以 DNG 灰掉；导出面板会在灰掉的选项旁写明理由。
  • 合成的成片不能写回素材所在的文件夹：否则下次对同一个文件夹做堆栈，会把成片自己也叠进去。
  • 「与原文件同一位置」对临时打开的文件灰掉，原因同第 5 条。
  • 文件名撞了会自动加 _2、_3，绝不覆盖已有文件。
  • ⌥⇧⌘E「用上次设置导出」会沿用上次的位置、格式与命名，直接入队。

11. 「覆盖原文件」安全吗？

它在导出面板「位置」菜单的最底下，选中后整块变红，按钮变成「覆盖并导出 N 张」。请记住：

  • 不可撤销：文件夹里没有回收站，原来的像素不会留下副本；
  • 只对 HEIF 与 JPEG 照片开放，容器跟随原文件；RAW、实况照片、视频与合成成片都不能覆盖；
  • 它永远不会被记成「上次的设置」，⌥⇧⌘E 不会悄悄覆盖你的原片。

12. 导入 LUT 有什么要求？

  • 格式是 .cube，三维表边长 2 到 128；
  • 滤镜库只收 Rec.709 输入的风格化 LUT，最多导入 36 条（内置的「冰蓝」「负片」不占名额），置顶最多 5 条；
  • Apple Log / Apple Log 2 这类输入还原表是另一套清单，在视频编辑的「输入还原」里导入，别导进滤镜库；
  • 导入失败的原因会在「滤镜库」窗口里提示，没看到提示时可以打开这个窗口（⇧⌘L）再导入一次。

13. 视频能做什么？

  • 逐帧走带：空格播放 / 暂停，, . 逐帧，J K L 倒放 / 暂停 / 快进，Home End 跳到头尾；预览速度 0.25× 到 2×（不影响导出）。
  • 四层滤镜同样适用于视频（光影层的「画面上拖动调曲线」只给照片）。
  • Apple Log 与 Apple Log 2 素材：在「输入还原」里先挂还原表，再调色。
  • 缩放、并排对比、全屏和照片一样。
  • 导出为 HEVC，容器跟随原文件（MOV 出 MOV，MP4 出 MP4）。
  • 没有剪辑、裁切和时间线，视频一段一段单独导出。

14. 版权与署名会写到哪里？

在侧栏「资源 › 版权与署名」里打开「写入版权与署名」（默认关闭），填上作者，之后导出的成片会带上作者、版权声明、联系方式与使用条款：

  • 照片写进 TIFF 的 Artist / Copyright 与 IPTC 字段；织光自己写出的 DNG 另带一份 XMP；
  • 视频与延时写进 QuickTime 的作者与版权字段；
  • 作者为空时一个字段都不写。

单张与批量导出的照片、视频导出、堆栈的 HEIC/TIFF/PNG 会保留原片里的 GPS 位置。分享前如果不想带出拍摄地点，请先检查元数据——织光目前没有「导出时去掉位置」的选项。详见隐私政策第 5 条。

16. 怎么彻底清空织光的数据？

  1. 删除织光本体；
  2. 删除 ~/Library/Containers/com.gwongsam.rayloom/——设置、署名、滤镜库、预设、导入的还原表、缩略图缓存都在里面；
  3. 如果还想清掉编辑配方，删除素材文件夹里以点开头的 .crrecipe 隐藏文件（访达里 ⇧⌘. 显示）。

试用与购买记录属于你的 Apple 账户，不会因此消失，重装后「恢复购买」即可。

2. How does the trial work? What about reinstalling or a new Mac?

  • The trial lasts 14 days, counted from the moment you click 开始 14 天免费试用 (Start 14-day free trial). Nothing counts down until you do.
  • The trial is an in-app purchase priced at zero, so it belongs to your Apple Account: one per account. Reinstalling or moving to another Mac does not restart it.
  • After it ends, browsing, comparing, and editing stay free; only exporting needs the Full Version. Jobs already running in the queue are not affected.
  • On a new Mac or after reinstalling, sign in with the same Apple Account and click 恢复购买 (Restore Purchases) in the Full Version window. If it says this Apple Account hasn't bought the Full Version, check that the App Store is signed in with the account you purchased with.
  • "Waiting for approval" means the purchase needs a parent or administrator to approve it; the app unlocks automatically once approved.

3. Which formats are supported? My RAW won't open

TypeExtensions
Camera RAWDNG, NEF, CR2, CR3, ARW, RAF, RW2, ORF

VideoMOV, MP4

RAW files are decoded by macOS itself; Rayloom has no decoder of its own. Whether a camera's RAW files can be developed depends on whether your version of macOS supports that model — new cameras often need a system update. Try opening the same file in Preview: if Preview can't open it, Rayloom can't either.

Rayloom only lists the top level of a folder: it doesn't look inside subfolders and skips hidden files.

4. Do edits change my originals? Where are recipes stored?

No. Every adjustment in the four layers (lens filters, tone, LUT, color) is saved as a hidden recipe file next to the original: same name, a leading dot, and the .crrecipe extension (for example .IMG_0001.crrecipe next to IMG_0001.heic). The original is left untouched.

  • Press ⇧⌘. in Finder to see hidden files. Copy the whole folder to another Mac and the recipes come along.
  • Pressing 复位 (Reset) while editing, or bringing every setting back to its default, deletes the recipe file.
  • Only exporting creates new files, and an original is only rewritten if you explicitly choose "Overwrite original" in the export panel (see question 11).

5. My edits to files opened with "Open Files" disappeared

That's by design. ⌘O Open Files and double-clicking in Finder open files temporarily: no path is remembered, no bookmark is kept, and nothing is left behind when the window closes. The macOS sandbox grants Rayloom access to those files only, not the folder they're in, so their recipes can't be written next to them and are kept in memory until you quit.

To keep your edits, add the whole folder as a source with 添加文件夹… (Add Folder…, ⇧⌘O), or export the photos before quitting.

6. A folder in the sidebar is greyed out

A greyed-out folder marked "location temporarily unavailable" usually means an external drive is disconnected, a network volume isn't mounted, or an iCloud Drive folder hasn't downloaded yet. Rayloom keeps the entry and checks again automatically when you switch back to it.

If the folder was moved or renamed, right-click it in the sidebar, choose 从列表中移除 (Remove from List), and add it again. Removing only deletes the entry — never any file in the folder.

7. What material do stacks, time-lapses, and focus stacks need?

Select two or more items in the grid, and four workflows appear at the top of the inspector: 堆栈 (Stack), 延时 (Time-lapse), 景深 (Focus), 导出 (Export).

WorkflowSuitable materialOptions
StackThe same scene shot in sequence from a tripod, 2 to 1,500 frames of the same sizeBlend: Average (noise reduction, smoothing water and crowds) / Maximum (star trails, light trails, fireworks) / Star sky (rotational alignment on the stars); turn on image alignment for handheld shots or a slightly moving camera
Time-lapseA set of photos shot at intervals, enough for at least 2 framesPlain / noise-reduction stack / clean-up stack / star-trail video; 1080p or 4K; 30 or 60 fps. Frames are ordered by capture time
FocusMatching DNG files only: a focus sweep from one camera with the same size and orientationFrames are taken as near-to-far by default; if you shot far-to-near, turn on that option. The order follows the grid's sort order

The noise-reduction and clean-up stacks assume a fixed camera; time-lapses shot with a moving camera will ghost, so use the plain time-lapse for those.

8. Why were some frames excluded?

After you pick a workflow, Rayloom checks the selection first. Anything that can't join the sequence is labelled with the reason right on its grid cell, and the inspector shows how many of the selected items can be used. Common reasons:

  • Video — combining only takes photos;
  • Different size — it doesn't match the size of most frames (a cropped image slipped in, for example);
  • Same shot as another file — when a RAW and a JPEG share a name, only the RAW is kept;
  • Size unreadable / unsupported format — the file is damaged, or macOS can't decode that RAW;
  • Frames larger than 100 megapixels are also refused.

9. Why doesn't my combined result have my color edits?

Stacks, time-lapses, and focus stacks read the original material and don't apply each photo's recipe — with dozens of frames each edited differently, there's no single right choice. The result appears under 成片 (Results) in the sidebar once exported: open it and edit it with the four layers as usual.

To burn one look into a batch of photos, use the Export workflow and, under 配方 (Recipe) in the export panel, choose to apply one recipe to all — from a preset or copied from a photo.

10. Which export format should I pick? Why is DNG greyed out?

FormatWhen to use it
HEIF / HEICFor viewing and sharing directly; the smallest files (lossy). Batch exports offer 8-bit and 10-bit, with 10-bit the default when RAW files are included
TIFF · 16-bitFor handing off to another app for further editing, with more tonal headroom
PNGWhen you need lossless and widely compatible
DNG · linear negativeCombined results only. Stacks need all frames to be matching RAW files (or RAW plus TIFF) — any JPEG, HEIC, or PNG in the mix rules it out; focus stacks take DNG input anyway
  • Single and batch exports produce display-referred images, so DNG is greyed out there; the export panel states the reason next to any greyed-out option.
  • Combined results can't be written back into the source folder; otherwise the next stack of that folder would include the result itself.
  • "Same location as the original" is greyed out for temporarily opened files, for the reason in question 5.
  • Name clashes get _2, _3 appended — existing files are never overwritten.
  • ⌥⇧⌘E (export with last settings) reuses the last location, format, and naming, and queues the export straight away.

11. Is "Overwrite original" safe?

It sits at the bottom of the location menu in the export panel. When chosen, the section turns red and the button reads "Overwrite and export N". Keep in mind:

  • It can't be undone: there is no trash for folders, and the original pixels are not kept anywhere;
  • it's only available for HEIF and JPEG photos, keeping the original container; RAW files, Live Photos, videos, and combined results can't be overwritten;
  • it is never remembered as your last settings, so ⌥⇧⌘E can never silently overwrite originals.

12. What LUTs can I import?

  • .cube files with a 3D size between 2 and 128;
  • the filter library takes creative LUTs that expect Rec.709 input: up to 36 imported (the built-in 冰蓝 and 负片 don't count), with up to 5 pinned;
  • input-restore LUTs for Apple Log or Apple Log 2 footage go in a separate list: import them under 输入还原 (Input Restore) when editing a video, not into the filter library;
  • import errors are reported in the filter library window; if you didn't see one, open that window (⇧⌘L) and import again.

13. What can I do with video?

  • Frame-accurate transport: space to play or pause, , . to step frames, J K L for reverse, pause, and forward, Home End for start and end; preview speeds from 0.25× to 2× (exports aren't affected).
  • The four edit layers work on video too (dragging on the picture to adjust the curve is for photos only).
  • Apple Log and Apple Log 2 footage: attach a restore LUT under Input Restore first, then grade.
  • Zoom, side-by-side compare, and full screen work just like photos.
  • Exports are HEVC, keeping the original container (MOV to MOV, MP4 to MP4).
  • There's no trimming, cropping, or timeline; videos are exported one at a time.

14. Where are copyright and credit written?

In the sidebar under 资源 › 版权与署名 (Resources › Copyright), turn on writing copyright and credit (off by default) and enter an author. Results exported afterwards carry the author, copyright notice, contact details, and usage terms:

  • photos get TIFF Artist / Copyright and IPTC fields; DNG files written by Rayloom also get XMP;
  • videos and time-lapses get QuickTime author and copyright fields;
  • with no author entered, nothing is written.

Single and batch photo exports, video exports, and stacks saved as HEIC/TIFF/PNG keep the GPS location from the original. Check the metadata before sharing if you'd rather not reveal where a photo was taken — Rayloom currently has no option to strip location on export. See section 5 of the Privacy Policy.

16. How do I remove all of Rayloom's data?

  1. Delete the Rayloom app;
  2. delete ~/Library/Containers/com.gwongsam.rayloom/ — settings, credits, the filter library, presets, imported restore LUTs, and the thumbnail cache all live there;
  3. to remove edit recipes too, delete the hidden .crrecipe files (leading dot) in your photo folders (press ⇧⌘. in Finder to show them).

Trial and purchase records belong to your Apple Account and are not affected; after reinstalling, use Restore Purchases.
