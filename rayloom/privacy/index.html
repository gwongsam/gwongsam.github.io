1. 谁在提供这款应用

织光（英文名 Rayloom，下称「本应用」）由个人开发者 gwongsam（下称「我们」）开发与发行，运行于 Mac。它用来浏览、编辑与导出照片和视频，并把多张素材合成为星空堆栈、延时视频或景深合成的成片。

本政策说明本应用如何处理你的信息。贯穿全文的前提是：本应用不联网，也没有后端服务器，你的照片、视频与编辑内容不会到达我们手中。下文描述的，实际是数据如何在你自己的 Mac 上流动。

2. 应用处理哪些数据

以下数据在你使用过程中产生或被读取，全部保留在你的 Mac 上：

类别具体内容
你选中的照片与视频为了显示缩略图与预览、编辑、合成和导出而读取的像素与元数据——包括原片自带的拍摄时间、相机与镜头、曝光参数，以及 GPS 位置（如果原片里有）
编辑配方你在镜前、光影、LUT、调色四层里调过的参数
资源配方预设、滤镜库（含你导入的 .cube 文件）、你导入的视频输入还原表
版权与署名你在「版权与署名」窗口里填写的作者、版权声明、联系方式与使用条款
设置与记忆素材来源文件夹清单、最近用过的导出位置、各玩法上次选的导出格式与命名方式、界面偏好
授权状态是否开始了试用、何时开始、是否已购买完整版——从 Apple 的交易记录读出后在本机缓存一份结论（见第 6 条）

本应用不索取、不收集：身份证件号码、手机号码、支付信息、通讯录、日历、广告标识符、设备指纹，也不会主动获取你的位置（原片里自带的 GPS 只随你的文件一起被处理，见第 5 条）。本应用没有账号体系，无需注册或登录。

3. 数据存放在哪里

全部在你自己的 Mac 上，分三处：

  • 本应用的沙盒容器（~/Library/Containers/com.gwongsam.rayloom/）。设置、署名、滤镜库、预设、授权状态缓存都在这里；缩略图缓存放在其中的 Caches 目录，系统可以随时清理，清掉后会按需重建。
  • 原片旁边。编辑配方存成一个与原片同名、以点开头的隐藏文件，扩展名 .crrecipe（例如 IMG_0001.heic 旁边的 .IMG_0001.crrecipe）。这样配方跟着文件走，把整个文件夹拷到另一台 Mac 上，配方还在。通过「打开文件」临时打开的文件例外：它们的配方只留在内存里，退出本应用即清空，不写入任何文件。
  • 你选的导出位置。导出与合成的成片只写到你在导出面板里指定的地方。

本应用不使用 iCloud 同步，不向任何服务器写入数据。

4. 文件访问与系统权限

  • 沙盒。本应用运行在 macOS 的应用沙盒里，只能读写你亲自选中的文件和文件夹——通过「添加文件夹」「打开文件」、导出面板里选择位置、从访达拖入或双击打开。
  • 书签。为了下次启动不必重新授权，本应用会为你添加的来源文件夹和导出位置保存 macOS 标准的「安全范围书签」，存在沙盒容器里。在侧栏里移除某个文件夹，即删除这条记录；这不会碰文件夹里的任何文件。临时打开的文件不保存书签。
  • 不请求其他权限。本应用不请求相机、麦克风、照片图库、位置、通讯录、日历或任何其他系统权限。
  • 没有网络权限。本应用的沙盒配置没有开启网络访问（既不能发起、也不能接收连接）。窗口里的「隐私政策」「用户协议」链接由系统默认浏览器打开，不经过本应用。

5. 导出的文件里带着什么

成片里带不带原片的元数据，取决于它是怎么出来的：

成片沿用原片的元数据
单张与批量导出的照片、视频导出沿用原片的描述性元数据：拍摄时间、相机与镜头、曝光参数、IPTC，以及原片里如果有的 GPS 位置
堆栈的 HEIC、TIFF、PNG沿用参考帧的元数据，含 GPS（如有）；曝光时间写成累计曝光
堆栈与景深合成的 DNG带 EXIF 拍摄参数，不带 GPS
景深合成的 HEIF、TIFF、PNG，以及延时视频不带原片的 EXIF 与 GPS

此外，每一份成片都会写入：

  • 处理软件的名称与版本（「织光 版本号」），以及表示「已重新渲染」的标准标记；
  • 如果你在「版权与署名」里打开了「写入版权与署名」（默认关闭）并填了作者，作者、版权声明、联系方式与使用条款也会写进文件。

这些信息跟着文件一起走。把成片发给别人或上传到网上之前，如果不想带出拍摄地点或联系方式，请先检查文件的元数据。本应用目前没有「导出时去掉位置」的选项。

6. 试用与购买

  • 本应用可免费下载；浏览、对比与编辑一直免费，导出与合成需要「织光完整版」。
  • 「14 天试用」是一个价格为 0 的 App 内购买项目，「织光完整版」是一次性买断、不自动续费。二者都由 Apple 通过 App Store 完成，我们看不到你的 Apple 账户、支付方式或账单信息。

7. 诊断与崩溃数据

本应用不内置任何崩溃统计或性能分析 SDK，也不会自行回传运行日志——它本来也没有联网能力。

9. 数据保留与删除

我们不持有你的数据，因此不存在由我们进行的「保留期」。数据的存续完全由你控制：

  • 来源文件夹：在侧栏里移除即可，只移除记录与书签，不碰文件夹里的文件。
  • 编辑配方：在编辑时按「复位」，或把参数都调回初始状态，对应的配方文件会被删掉；也可以直接删除原片旁边的隐藏 .crrecipe 文件（在访达里按 ⇧⌘. 显示隐藏文件）。
  • 滤镜库、配方预设：在各自的窗口里删除；版权与署名：在「版权与署名」窗口里清空，或关掉「写入版权与署名」。

  • 试用与购买记录：属于你的 Apple 账户，由 Apple 管理。

你无需联系我们即可完成上述全部操作，我们也没有任何副本可供删除。

Privacy Policy

For the Rayloom app for MacEffective: 14 September 2026Last updated: 14 September 2026

The short version

  • Rayloom doesn't go online. Its sandbox has no network access, so it cannot reach any server — and we don't run one.
  • It only touches the files you point it at. Rayloom can read only the folders you add, the files you open, and the photos and videos you drag in or double-click from Finder. All processing happens on your Mac.
  • Your originals stay as they are. Edits are saved as a hidden recipe file next to the original. An original is only rewritten if you explicitly choose "Overwrite original" in the export panel's location menu (HEIF and JPEG photos only).
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics SDKs, no trackers, no third-party code. The trial and purchase are handled by Apple; we never see your account or payment details.

1. Who provides this app

Rayloom (Chinese name 织光, "the app") is developed and published by gwongsam ("we"), an independent developer, for Mac. It is used to browse, edit, and export photos and videos, and to combine many frames into a star stack, a time-lapse video, or a focus stack.

This policy explains how the app handles your information. One premise runs through all of it: the app does not connect to the internet and has no backend server, so your photos, videos, and edits never reach us. What follows describes how data moves around your own Mac.

2. What data the app handles

The following is created or read as you use the app, and all of it stays on your Mac:

CategoryWhat it covers
Photos and videos you choosePixels and metadata read to show thumbnails and previews, and to edit, combine, and export — including the capture time, camera and lens, exposure settings, and GPS location if the original contains one
Edit recipesThe settings you adjust in the four layers: Lens, Light, LUT, and Color
ResourcesRecipe presets, the filter library (including .cube files you import), and the input-restore LUTs you import for video
Copyright and creditThe author, copyright notice, contact details, and usage terms you enter in the Copyright window
SettingsYour source folders, recent export locations, the last export format and naming used for each workflow, and interface preferences
License stateWhether a trial has started, when, and whether the full version was purchased — read from Apple's transaction records and cached on your Mac (see section 6)

The app does not request or collect government ID numbers, phone numbers, payment information, contacts, calendars, advertising identifiers, or device fingerprints, and it never asks for your location (GPS data already inside your originals is only processed along with your files — see section 5). There is no account system and nothing to sign up for.

3. Where the data lives

Entirely on your Mac, in three places:

  • The app's sandbox container (~/Library/Containers/com.gwongsam.rayloom/). Settings, credits, the filter library, presets, and the cached license state live here. Thumbnails are cached in its Caches folder, which the system may clear at any time; they are rebuilt as needed.
  • Next to your originals. An edit recipe is saved as a hidden file with the original's name, a leading dot, and the .crrecipe extension (for example .IMG_0001.crrecipe next to IMG_0001.heic). The recipe travels with the file: copy the whole folder to another Mac and your edits come along. Files opened with "Open Files" are the exception — their recipes are kept in memory only and discarded when you quit, and nothing is written to disk.
  • The export location you pick. Exported and combined results are written only where you choose in the export panel.

The app does not use iCloud sync and writes nothing to any server.

4. File access and permissions

  • Sandbox. The app runs in the macOS App Sandbox and can only read and write files and folders you choose yourself — via Add Folder, Open Files, choosing a location in the export panel, dragging from Finder, or double-clicking.
  • Bookmarks. So you don't have to grant access again after a restart, the app stores standard macOS security-scoped bookmarks for the source folders and export locations you add, inside its sandbox container. Removing a folder from the sidebar deletes that record and never touches the files inside. Files opened temporarily get no bookmark.
  • No other permissions. The app does not request camera, microphone, Photos library, location, contacts, calendar, or any other system permission.
  • No network permission. The app's sandbox has network access switched off, both outgoing and incoming. The Privacy Policy and Terms links in its windows open in your default browser, not in the app.

5. What exported files carry

Whether a result carries your originals' metadata depends on how it was made:

ResultMetadata kept from the originals
Single and batch photo exports, video exportsThe descriptive metadata of the original: capture time, camera and lens, exposure settings, IPTC, and the GPS location if there is one
Stacks saved as HEIC, TIFF, or PNGThe reference frame's metadata, including GPS if present; exposure time is written as the total exposure
Stacks and focus stacks saved as DNGEXIF shooting data, without GPS
Focus stacks saved as HEIF, TIFF, or PNG, and time-lapse videosNone of the originals' EXIF or GPS

In addition, every result gets:

  • the processing software name and version ("Rayloom" and its version), plus the standard flag marking the image as re-rendered;
  • if you turned on "Write copyright and credit" in the Copyright window (off by default) and entered an author: the author, copyright notice, contact details, and usage terms.

This information travels with the file. Before sending results to someone or posting them online, check the metadata if you'd rather not share where a photo was taken or how to reach you. The app currently has no option to strip location on export.

6. Trial and purchase

  • The app is free to download. Browsing, comparing, and editing are always free; exporting and combining require Rayloom Full Version.
  • The "14-day Trial" is an in-app purchase priced at zero, and the Full Version is a one-time purchase that never renews. Both are completed by Apple through the App Store, and we never see your Apple Account, payment method, or billing details.
  • The app uses Apple's StoreKit on your Mac to read the transaction records, solely to determine whether the trial has started and how many days are left, and whether the Full Version was purchased. It caches that conclusion locally so a purchased app doesn't flash "Full Version required" at launch.
  • Trial and purchase records belong to your Apple Account. After reinstalling or moving to another Mac, sign in with the same account and choose Restore Purchases. Reinstalling does not restart the trial.
  • Refunds, Ask to Buy approvals, and similar matters are handled by Apple under its own rules.

7. Diagnostics and crash data

The app contains no crash-reporting or performance-analytics SDK and never sends logs anywhere on its own — it has no network capability to do so.

If you have turned on "Share with app developers" under System Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple may provide developers with aggregated, de-identified crash reports and performance metrics. That data is collected and de-identified by Apple, contains none of your photos or edits, and can be turned off in System Settings at any time.

8. Third parties: what we don't do

Spelled out, to avoid ambiguity:

  • We do not sell your personal information (including "sale" and "sharing" as defined under US state laws);
  • We do not use or disclose your information for targeted advertising; the app contains no ad SDK;
  • We integrate no analytics, event-tracking, A/B testing, crash-reporting, attribution, or profiling service;
  • The app contains no third-party SDKs — only Apple's own system frameworks;
  • We do not use your photos, videos, or edits to train any model, and we perform no automated decision-making;
  • We neither receive nor transmit any of your information, so there is no cross-border transfer initiated by us.

The one third party involved is Apple, which handles in-app purchases and transaction records through the App Store. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

9. Retention and deletion

We hold none of your data, so there is no retention period on our side. How long anything lives is entirely up to you:

  • Source folders: remove them from the sidebar. Only the record and bookmark are removed; the files are untouched.
  • Edit recipes: press Reset while editing, or bring every setting back to its default, and the recipe file is deleted; you can also delete the hidden .crrecipe file next to the original yourself (press ⇧⌘. in Finder to show hidden files).
  • Filter library and recipe presets: delete them in their windows; copyright and credit: clear them in the Copyright window, or turn off "Write copyright and credit".
  • Everything on this Mac (including imported input-restore LUTs and the thumbnail cache): delete the app and the folder ~/Library/Containers/com.gwongsam.rayloom/.
  • Files you exported: manage them yourself — we have no way of knowing where they are.
  • Trial and purchase records: they belong to your Apple Account and are managed by Apple.

None of this requires contacting us, and we have no copies to delete on your behalf.

12. Security

  • The app runs in the macOS App Sandbox, can access only the files you authorize, and is built with the system's Hardened Runtime.
  • The app has no network permission, so there is no path for your photos or edits to leave your Mac through it.
  • We hold none of your data, so there is no "our database was breached" risk to speak of.
  • Exported files carry your originals' metadata (see section 5) — keep that in mind when sharing.

That said: the security of your Mac underpins all of the above. Use a login password, turn on FileVault, and keep macOS updated. No technical measure can guarantee absolute security.
