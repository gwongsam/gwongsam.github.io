它是什么

What it is

选了什么，就出什么
What you pick is what you get
ProRAW、Bayer RAW、HEIF 任选，分辨率与位深自己定。快门、ISO、白平衡、对焦全手动，也可以只手动其中一项。这台机器、这枚镜头拍不了的格式，会直接标明不支持，而不是偷偷换一种拍。
ProRAW, Bayer RAW, or HEIF, with resolution and bit depth up to you. Shutter, ISO, white balance, and focus can each go manual on their own. Formats your device or lens can't actually produce are marked unsupported rather than quietly swapped for something else.
算出来的曝光
Exposures that are computed
长曝、星空、星轨、景深合成与延时收在「风光」一格里，边拍边合成，采集段与合成段随时能停。星空按星点对齐，星轨边拍边累积，景深一次扫焦前后都实。
Long exposure, star stacking, star trails, focus stacking, and time-lapse live under one mode, combining frames as they arrive. Both capture and merge can be stopped at any point. Stars are aligned by their own motion; a single focus sweep comes back sharp front to back.
按快门前就定调子
Set the look before the shutter
镜前那块玻璃（黑柔、白柔、星光镜、拉丝光）是算出来的，不是贴图；再叠光影、LUT 与调色盘，取景时就是成片的样子。相机与相册共用同一套滤镜。
The lens filters — diffusion, star, and anamorphic streak — are computed optics, not overlays. Stack tone curves, LUTs, and a color palette on top, and the viewfinder already shows the final look. The camera and the library share one filter stack.

价格与隐私

Pricing and privacy

拍摄、编辑、导出与分享一直免费。长曝、景深合成、堆栈与延时堆栈等七种算出来的玩法，每种免费 3 次完整成片、用完不过期；镜前滤镜与调色盘未解锁时可以实时取景，只是不落进成片。要一直用，可以买 30 天通行证、年度订阅或永久买断。没有账号、没有广告、没有统计，也没有我们自己的服务器：你的照片与编辑内容不会被上传。只有两处会联网，都是 Apple 的系统服务——查看带位置的照片时向 Apple 地图反查地名，以及由 App Store 处理购买。

Shooting, editing, exporting, and sharing are always free. Seven computed workflows — long exposure, focus stacking, library stacks, and time-lapse stacks — each include 3 free full-quality results that never expire. Lens filters and the color palette stay live in the viewfinder until unlocked; they just don't reach the saved file. To keep using them, there's a 30-day pass, a yearly subscription, or a one-time purchase. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no servers of ours: your photos and edits are never uploaded. Only two things reach the network, both Apple system services — looking up a place name for a geotagged photo, and purchases handled by the App Store.
